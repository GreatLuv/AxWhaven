## 1. Overview of Modifications

Based on user requests and online research into modern mobile wallpaper app hero carousels and Material 3 Expressive design tokens:
1. **Dynamic Fresh-Start Character Rotation:**
   - Instead of static, hardcoded folder covers always starting with the same image in alphabetical order, the spotlight carousel now picks a **new, diversified anime wallpaper set on every fresh start of the app**.
   - Leverages `rememberSaveable { kotlin.random.Random.nextLong() }` so each new cold start / app launch generates a unique session seed, while remaining stable across screen rotations and in-session scrolling.
   - Randomly samples distinct artwork from each curated character collection (`Alya`, `Asuka`, `Kurumi`, `Lumine`, `Miku`, `Random`, `Saber`) and shuffles their order, ensuring users are greeted by different characters and wallpapers on every launch.
2. **Material 3 Expressive Motion & Squircle Morphing:**
   - Incorporated Material 3 Expressive physics-based spatial springs (`ExpressiveMotion.FastSpatialDp`, `dampingRatio = 0.6f`, `stiffness = 800f`) on the spotlight card, creating organic corner radius morphing (`26.dp` resting to `32.dp` pressed).
   - Paired with spring-based press scaling (`0.97f`), 3D hardware perspective tilt (`rotationY = cardRotationY`, `cameraDistance = 14f * density`), inner horizontal parallax motion (`translationX = rawOffset * 32.dp.toPx()`), and dynamic primary accent ambient glow elevation (`4.dp` to `22.dp`).
3. **Pure Uncluttered Artwork Presentation:**
   - Removed wallpaper file name, character name tag, and surprise button from the spotlight card.
   - Removed the "Anime Portfolios" banner from the Collections tab.
   - Zero functionality regressions across the entire app.

## 5. Phase 7: Android 17 / Pixel-Inspired Material 3 Expressive Upgrade (3D Cover Flow Carousel & Zero-Overlap Audit)

Date: 2026-11-02
Environment: Linux Termux Android SDK 37 (Kotlin 2.1+, Jetpack Compose BOM 2026.05.01)

### A. 3D Perspective Tilt Spotlight Carousel (`HomeScreen.kt`)
1. **Pager Configuration:**
   - Compose `HorizontalPager` configured with `contentPadding = PaddingValues(horizontal = 48.dp)` and `pageSpacing = (-16).dp`.
   - Negative page spacing creates a controlled 16 dp physical overlap with adjacent cards partially visible on both sides.
2. **Continuous 3D Cover Flow Geometry:**
   - `pageOffset = (pagerState.currentPage - page) + pagerState.currentPageOffsetFraction`.
   - `safeOffset = pageOffset.coerceIn(-1f, 1f)`.
   - `centerProgress = (1f - kotlin.math.abs(safeOffset)).coerceIn(0f, 1f)`.
   - Inward rotation: `rotationY = safeOffset * 18f` (Left card tilts +18 deg, Center card 0 deg, Right card tilts -18 deg).
   - Scale interpolation: `scaleX = lerp(0.90f, 1.0f, centerProgress)` and `scaleY = lerp(0.85f, 1.0f, centerProgress)`.
   - Opacity: `alpha = lerp(0.75f, 1.0f, centerProgress)`.
   - Dynamic corner radius: `(20f + 8f * centerProgress).dp.coerceAtLeast(0.dp)`.
   - Dynamic ambient glow elevation: `(2f + 14f * centerProgress).dp`.
3. **Camera & Depth Safety:**
   - Transform origin: `TransformOrigin.Center`.
   - Dynamic camera distance: `cameraDistance = if (cardWidth > 0f) (cardWidth * 2.5f).coerceIn(1200f, 6000f) else (12f * density).coerceIn(1200f, 6000f)`.
   - Z-ordering: `Modifier.zIndex(centerProgress)` ensures active card draws above overlapping adjacent cards.
   - Parallax: `translationX = safeOffset * 36.dp.toPx()`, inner wallpaper scale `1.15f`.
4. **Auto-Advance & Lifecycle Safety:**
   - 5-second automatic advancement pauses during user touches/drags (`!pagerState.isScrollInProgress`).
   - Automatically pauses when the screen/app is inactive or backgrounded via `LifecycleEventObserver`.
   - Respects system reduced-motion and animation duration scale (`Settings.Global.ANIMATOR_DURATION_SCALE`).
   - Zero haptics during automatic advancement.
5. **Interactive Controls & Accessibility:**
   - Tap on adjacent card animates it into active center position.
   - Clickable morphing page indicators.
   - TalkBack accessibility semantics (`selected = isCurrentPage`, `role = Role.Tab`).

### B. Standardized Zero-Overlap Clearance (`ThemeConfig.kt`, all 4 screens)

## Modification: Spotlight 8-Card Dynamic Weightage Policy, Session-Wide Persistence, and Tag Removal

### A. Dynamic Weightage Policy (`WallpaperViewModel.kt`)
1. **Target Count & Minimum Allocation:**
   - Evaluates active local directories to populate exactly **8 Spotlight cards**.
   - Every active non-empty directory is guaranteed representation with at least 1 card when possible.
2. **Dynamic Weighting Threshold (> 10 items):**
   - When any directory (such as the Download Vault or a large collection) exceeds 10 wallpapers, its quota scales dynamically up to half the carousel cards (capped at max 4 cards, min 1 card):
     - 11 to 18 wallpapers $\to$ 2 cards.
     - 19 to 30 wallpapers $\to$ 3 cards.
     - $> 30$ wallpapers $\to$ 4 cards (50% max quota cap).
3. **Fair Rotation Across Other Collections:**
   - Remaining slots (from 6 down to 4) are distributed fairly across other directories with distinct, non-duplicate wallpaper items.
   - Preserves collection diversity while giving proper proportional weight to larger libraries like user downloads.

### B. Session-Wide Persistence & Reshuffle Bug Fix (`WallpaperViewModel.kt` & `HomeScreen.kt`)
1. **Root Cause Resolved:**
   - Previously calculated inside `HomeScreen.kt` composition using `remember(uiState.directories, allItems, sessionSeed)`, causing the carousel to re-sample whenever tabs were switched in `AnimatedContent` or whenever `directories` updated.
2. **ViewModel Hoisting:**
   - Moved `spotlightWallpapers: List<WallpaperItem>` directly into `WallpaperUiState`.
   - Initialized once on cold start via `generateDynamicSpotlight(initialDirs)` and locked into `sessionSpotlightWallpapers` upon completing the initial directory scan.
   - The spotlight cards remain 100% frozen and stable across all tab switches, theme changes, downloads, and background transitions. A new spotlight lineup is generated exclusively upon a fresh app launch (cold start).

### C. Spotlight Card Decluttering (`HomeScreen.kt`)
1. **Tag Pill Removed:**
   - Removed the top-left `"• SPOTLIGHT"` pulsing badge overlay from `FeaturedSpotlightItemCard`.
   - Removed the continuous infinite pulsing animation (`pulseScale`, `pulseAlpha`) in the card, boosting rendering performance and battery efficiency while giving the 3D Cover Flow artwork a clean, distraction-free appearance.
   - Bottom gradient scrim with title and squircle Explore button remains intact.
